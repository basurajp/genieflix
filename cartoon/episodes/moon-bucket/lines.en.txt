# a tiny cartoon — written for the ear: short phrases, real pauses
One summer night, little Anu looked into the water bucket... and gasped.
Dadu! Come quick! The moon fell into our bucket!
And there it was. Round. Silver. Floating in the dark water.
Don't worry, little moon. I will save you!
She reached in... and the moon shattered, into a hundred silver ripples.
Oh no. Oh no, no, no! I broke it! Dadu, I broke the moon!
Hmm. That is serious. A broken moon. Let me see.
Grandfather looked into the bucket. Then up, at the sky. Then back, into the bucket.
Ah! Good news, Anu. Wait for the water... to sit still.
So they waited. The frog watched too. Nobody breathed.
Slowly... slowly... the water calmed. And the moon stitched itself back together.
It healed! Dadu, look! I fixed the moon!
You did. And the real one never left the sky, little one. Some things only break... in a hurry.
That night, Anu fell asleep watching two moons. One in the sky. One in a bucket... guarded by a frog.
