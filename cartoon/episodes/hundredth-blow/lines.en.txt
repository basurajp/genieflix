# motivational story — written for the ear
There was a rock on the mountain... that everyone had given up on.
Everyone... except a young stonecutter, named Kabir.
Every stone breaks. This one is just... taking its time.
He struck it once. Nothing. He struck it ten times. Nothing.
Give it up, boy. Some things... never break.
Fifty blows. Not even a scratch. His arms burned. His hands shook.
One more. Just... one more.
Ninety-eight. Ninety-nine. Still... nothing.
And then came... the hundredth blow.
The rock split. Clean. In two.
The village came running. They called it a miracle.
It wasn't the last blow that broke it. It was every blow before.
Remember that, on the days when nothing seems to move.
Because nothing is ever wasted... when you refuse to stop.
